PDF Toolkit

Merge, split, organise, compress, sign, scan and read PDFs. Every job runs on your phone — there is no server to upload to.

What it does

  • Merge several documents into one, in the order you set.
  • Split a document, taking out the pages or ranges you name.
  • Organise pages — reorder, rotate, delete.
  • Compress a document, or a batch of them, to a size you can send.
  • Sign a page with your own signature, placed where you want it.
  • Scan paper with the camera; edges, perspective and lighting handled for you.
  • Photos to PDF — pick pictures, get one document.
  • Pull the text out of a scan, and copy or save it.

Nothing is uploaded

Every operation happens on the device. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own, so your documents have nowhere to go but the folder you save them to.

Pro

The free version does every job, with limits on how much at once. Pro lifts those limits and adds signing and text extraction. It is one payment, not a subscription, and it is tied to your Google account, so it comes back when you reinstall.
