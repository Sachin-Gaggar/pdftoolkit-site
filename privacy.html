Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version

Offline PDF does not collect, transmit or store your personal data. Your documents stay on your device, and the app has no server to send them to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

  • A working copy of the files you pick. When you choose a document or a photo, the app copies it into a temporary workspace so it can be read while you work — the original is never modified. That workspace is cleared when you finish a job, and uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The result of a job. A merged, split, compressed, signed or scanned document is written to that same workspace until you save or share it. Nothing else is kept: the app has no library, no history and no index of what you have opened.

Where your results go

A finished document leaves the workspace only when you say so — by saving it to a folder you choose, or by handing it to Android's share sheet. What happens after that is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or location you chose.

The camera

The scanner uses Google Play Services' on-device document scanner. Pages are processed on your device and turned into a PDF locally. No image or document is sent to a server by this app.

Photos

Turning pictures into a PDF uses Android's photo picker. The app receives only the images you select, and reads no others.

Text recognition

Pulling the text out of a scan uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device. The pages and the recognised text are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock Pro. Your purchase is tied to your Google account, which is why it comes back automatically when you reinstall.

Accounts

There are none. The app has no sign-in and no user accounts.

Analytics and advertising

This version contains no analytics and no advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
